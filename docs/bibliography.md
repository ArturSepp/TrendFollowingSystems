# Bibliography

*Author: [Artur Sepp](https://github.com/ArturSepp)*

This is the reference list of the [trendfollowing documentation](https://github.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems).
Software citation: [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems/blob/main/CITATION.cff).

Every entry below is written once, in one style: authors, year, title, venue with volume, issue and
pages, then a DOI or a stable source link. A chapter's References section copies the entries it
cites verbatim and may append a sentence on what the work contributes to that chapter;
`tests/test_documentation_handbook.py` enforces the match. Add a new work here first, then cite it.

Each DOI below was checked against its Crossref record (authors, title, journal, volume and pages)
on 1 October 2026. An entry ending in `[pending publisher check]` has no DOI and was taken from the
bibliography of Sepp and Lucic (2026) or a standard record without a publisher-page check; remove
the mark once it has been checked.
